Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. Plain language, zero tracking.

1. Overview

At Mini Game Arcade, we respect your right to privacy. Our website is built as a completely static, client-side web application. We do not require accounts, we do not employ tracking pixels, and we do not sell your personal data to advertisers.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No Personal Identification: We do not ask for or collect names, phone numbers, home addresses, or payment information.
  • No Tracking Cookies: We do not place third-party advertising or analytics cookies on your browser.
  • No Third-Party Ad Networks: We do not run external ad networks that profile your browsing behavior.

3. Local Storage Usage

To make our games enjoyable, your browser stores minor game state information in HTML5 localStorage on your local device only:

  • Game Progression: Unlocked levels and star ratings in games like Cosmic Dodge.
  • High Scores: Your best scores in Fruit Slicer and Cosmic Dodge so you can beat your records.
  • Audio Preferences: Whether you have sound effects and background music turned on or off.

This data remains solely inside your browser and is never transmitted to any external server. You can wipe this data at any time by clearing your browser cache or using the in-game "Reset Data" option.

4. Contact Information

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this privacy policy, please visit our Contact page.
