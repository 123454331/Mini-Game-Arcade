🕹 FLIGHT & BLADE CONTROLS

  • Desktop Mouse: Click and drag your cursor rapidly across fruits to slice.
  • Mobile Touch: Swipe your finger across flying fruits. Multi-touch supported!
  • Pause: Press P or Esc, or tap the pause button in the top bar.

⭐ TIPS & COMBOS

  • Multi-Slice Combos: Slice 3 or more fruits in a single fluid swipe for bonus combo points!
  • Watch the Bombs: Slicing a bomb immediately costs a strike and breaks your rhythm.
  • 3 Strikes: Dropping 3 unsliced fruits ends the game. Stay focused!
