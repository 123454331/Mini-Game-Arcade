About Mini Game Arcade

Pure gameplay, crafted for the modern open web.

Our Mission

Mini Game Arcade was founded with a singular conviction: classic arcade gaming in the browser should be fast, delightful, and respect your attention and privacy.

Too many modern web game sites are bloated with multi-megabyte game engines, endless loading screens, intrusive pop-up banners, and aggressive trackers. We believe in crafting lightweight, handcrafted HTML5 Canvas games that launch in milliseconds and run buttery-smooth at 60 FPS on both low-power smartphones and high-refresh desktop monitors.

Our Technology Stack

  • Zero-Framework Frontend: Built using vanilla HTML5, modern CSS3, and ES6 JavaScript modules. No React or Angular overhead, ensuring instant Time-to-Interactive.
  • High-DPI 2D Canvas: Rendering routines scale cleanly with window.devicePixelRatio to render crisp vector graphics on Retina and mobile screens.
  • Procedural Web Audio API: Sound effects and musical loops are generated mathematically in real time. We do not download heavy MP3 audio assets.
  • Local Persistence: High scores, unlocked sectors, and player sound preferences are safely saved in your browser's localStorage.

Original Art & Integrity

All artwork, vector assets, entity designs, and sound effects across our games are 100% original. We do not use copyrighted assets from commercial franchises, and we never display fake testimonials or deceptive download links.
